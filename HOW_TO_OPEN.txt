PHILIPPINE LAND SERVICES GIS PORTAL - ONE WEBSITE

1. Extract the entire ZIP into a folder.
2. Double-click RUN_WEBSITE.bat (first run installs dependencies).
3. Your browser should open http://127.0.0.1:8000/ automatically.
4. Click Open EO79 or Open Harmonizer on the dashboard.
5. Keep the terminal window open while using the website.

For later runs, double-click START_WEBSITE.bat.
This is a LOCAL website, not a publicly deployed website.
Nationwide EO79 province data and slope database are NOT included.
The Climate module is a placeholder.
